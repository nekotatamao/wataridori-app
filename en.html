FAQ

Are the bird positions real?
The app does not track real birds. Positions are estimates calculated from each population's known breeding, stopover and wintering areas and when it typically arrives and leaves (its usual migration calendar). Real birds change their routes and timing with the weather and winds, and from year to year and bird to bird. See Settings → How Positions Are Estimated in the app.

How do I use it?
Drag to spin the globe, pinch to zoom and double-tap to zoom in. Tap a bird, or choose one from Birds, to see where it is likely to be today and its route through the year. Tap the date at the top to pick any date. Play Journey fast-forwards through the year.

What is Shuffle?
When no bird is selected, a few randomly chosen migrants fly in fast-forward. Tap Shuffle to pick a new set.

Where is "I've seen it" stored?
Only on your device. It is removed if you delete the app.

Does it work offline?
Yes, the globe and the guide work offline (ads need a connection).

There is too much motion
Turn on Settings → Reduce Motion to stop the globe's spinning, the wingbeats and the zoom animations.

How do I remove ads?
Buy Settings → Remove Ads (one-time purchase). After switching devices, use Restore Purchase on the same screen. Full-screen ads appear only occasionally when you close a guide page.

I saw an inappropriate ad
Use the ⓘ (AdChoices) mark in the corner of the ad to report it to Google, which serves the ads. You can also tell us the date and what the ad showed through the contact form below. Settings → Report an Inappropriate Ad in the app opens this page.

Map data
Land, lakes and borders on the globe come from Natural Earth (public domain). All bird illustrations and the globe are drawn by the app itself.

Birdsong
The birdsong that plays while you watch the globe uses public domain (CC0) recordings from Freesound. You can turn it off or change the volume in Settings.
